## Interpretation

Capture 005 provides independent descriptive evidence that the
approximately -5.2 kHz episode-median strongest-component observation
is repeatable under the tested controlled conditions.

At the same time, Episode Grouping V1 did not reproduce activation-count
concordance: six controlled activation markers produced five candidate
episodes.

These are separate results and neither is modified to improve the
appearance of the experiment.

This result does not establish:

- unique device identity
- between-device discrimination
- packet identity
- transmitter identity
- transmitter location
- a validated unique RF fingerprint

No classifier, identity threshold, or post-hoc spectral PASS tolerance
is introduced from Capture 005.
